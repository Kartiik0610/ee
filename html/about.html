About Echaar Equipments

Echaar Equipments Pvt. Ltd. is started with vision to offer expertise in making highly critical components involving difficult operations, by Industrial Engineering and Fixtures for various industries including ENERGY, Rolling Mills and Printing industries. At the helm of Echaar are a group of highly qualified technocrats with post graduate degree from renowned university. Their experience and knowledge helped Echaar achieving rapid growth in industrial engineering field.

Now in order to satisfy and delight our customer, as per requirements of the industries we have expanded our operations.

We are specialized in manufacturing of Assemblies, Sub-Assemblies, & Complete Equipments.

Our Capabilities

Comprehensive infrastructure equipped with advanced machinery and testing capabilities for high-precision manufacturing.

Machining

Heavy Fabrication
