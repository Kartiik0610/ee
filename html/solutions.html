ENGINEERING LIFECYCLE

The Echaar Turnkey Delivery Model

Our structured 6-phase engineering methodology ensures zero defect rates, total traceability, and seamless execution from initial concept review to on-site commissioning.

01

DFM & Engineering Consultation

Our team of postgraduate technocrats conducts in-depth Design for Manufacturability (DFM) reviews to optimize tolerances, material utilization, and production efficiency.

02

Certified Material Procurement

100% mill-tested forgings, castings, and heavy plates sourced exclusively from approved global steel mills, accompanied by full chemical and mechanical EN 10204 3.1 certification.

03

Heavy CNC Machining & Fabrication

Precision floor boring, 5-axis vertical machining, and certified ASME Section IX welding by qualified welders handling complex geometries up to 40+ Metric Tons.

04

Metrology & Certified NDT QA

Multi-stage quality gates using high-precision Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMM), Ultrasonic Testing (UT), Magnetic Particle (MPI), and hydrostatic pressure verification.

05

Shot Blasting & C5-M Marine Paint

Automated shot blasting followed by climate-controlled painting booths capable of executing certified C5-Marine anti-corrosion coating for extreme offshore longevity.

06

Integration, FAT & Global Logistics

Full turnkey mechanical and hydraulic integration, Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) with client inspectors, and seaworthy export packing for worldwide shipping.

INFRASTRUCTURE & CAPABILITIES

Core Manufacturing Pillars

Advanced heavy manufacturing machinery and accredited engineering practices dedicated to mission-critical precision.

⚙️

Large-Format Heavy CNC Machining

Heavy CNC Floor Borers with long travel axes, 5-Axis Vertical Machining Centers, and large diameter Vertical Turret Lathes capable of single-piece operations up to 40+ MT.

  • Boring travel up to 12,000 mm
  • Vertical turning diameter up to 5,500 mm
  • Precision micron repeatability
🔥

Certified Heavy Welding & Fabrication

Qualified welding processes adhering to ASME Section IX and ISO 3834 standards across carbon steels, stainless steels, alloy steels, and non-ferrous heavy fabrications.

  • SMAW, SAW, FCAW, TIG, MIG & MAG processes
  • Qualified WPS & PQR documentation
  • CNC thermal and plasma profile cutting
🛡️

Metrology & Advanced NDT Lab

Internal quality laboratory with Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMM), surface roughness testers, slip gauges, and ASNT/ISNT Level II certified NDT inspectors.

  • High-precision CMM dimensional verification
  • Ultrasonic, Magnetic Particle & Dye Penetrant
  • Positive Material Identification (PMI)
🎨

C5-Marine Blasting & Painting Booth

Dedicated in-house grit/shot blasting booth and temperature-controlled paint facility capable of applying high-build epoxy and polyurethane systems up to C5-M marine rating.

  • Surface preparation to SA 2.5 / SA 3.0
  • FROSIO / NACE certified paint inspectors
  • Offshore and subsea corrosion protection
🧩

Turnkey Assemblies & Integration

Specialized assembly bays equipped with heavy-duty overhead cranes for mechanical sub-assemblies, hydraulic piping fit-ups, drive mounting, and complete machinery integration.

  • Contract manufacturing of complete machines
  • Hydraulic piping and pressure testing
  • Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) with clients
📐

Industrial Fixture & Tooling Design

In-house engineering team developing custom heavy machining fixtures, specialized tooling setups, and process optimization to ensure repeatable micron accuracy on challenging geometries.

  • Custom fixture design for heavy components
  • Toolpath simulation and CAM optimization
  • Continuous Kaizen & cycle time reduction
