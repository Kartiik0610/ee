ENGINEERING STANDARDS

Built to Stringent Global Tolerances

Echaar Equipments integrates modern CNC machining, rigorous quality assurance, certified welding, and in-house coating to manufacture equipment that operates under extreme industrial environments.

⚙️

40+ Tons Capacity

Heavy lifting, large envelope CNC floor boring, and turning infrastructure capable of handling single components up to 40 Metric Tons.

📐

Micron Precision (CMM)

Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMM), master slip gauges, and laser optical alignment tools verifying geometric tolerances down to sub-microns.

🛡️

WPS / PQR Certified

Qualified welding processes including SMAW, SAW, FCAW, TIG, and MIG certified to ASME Section IX and international offshore standards.

🎨

C5 Marine Paint Shop

Controlled blasting booth and certified painting facilities providing C5-M extreme marine corrosion protection for global export requirements.
